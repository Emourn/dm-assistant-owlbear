## 2. FIXING VECNA — CORE RULES SUMMARY

These are the non-negotiable rules from the Fixing Vecna guide. **Do not contradict any of these:**

- **Start at Level 1.** Death House (Curse of Strahd) is the intro. Mists collect PCs from any setting.
- **Durst family are Vecna followers.** Tattoos on spirit hands. Never stated outright.
- **Nest of the Eldritch Eye** follows Death House (levels 3–4). Vecna appears, gives charms.
- **Remove Secrets Mechanic.** It's gone. No whispering secrets to the wind.
- **Remove Kas-as-Mordenkainen.** Mordenkainen is the real Mordenkainen. No Crown of Lies.
- **The Obelisk** replaces "shunting." 12' tall in Alustriel's sanctuary. Shards teleport to it.
- **Charms recharge** when Vecna appears. He appears repeatedly throughout the adventure. The stinging is in their hands (like Voldemort/Harry, but hands not forehead).
- **Three Wizards:** Alustriel (sanctuary owner, banned from Candlekeep), Mordenkainen (real, brings Circle of Eight), Zybilna (Tasha/Iggwilv, grants Wish spell later).
- **Wish spell** is tied to Infinite Staircase / Nafas vision showing Web's Edge.
- **Tomb of Horrors** (from Yawning Portal) replaces Tomb of Wayward Souls at level 16.
- **Hand and Eye of Vecna** found at level 16. Attunement requires self-mutilation.
- **Vecna archlich fight** at level 16 (manifestation, weak). **Vecna god fight** at level 20 (Whispered One homebrew stat block, NOT the WotC archlich).
- **Easy Mode:** Hand of Vecna + Rod of Seven Parts shifts Vecna's alignment to Lawful Good.
- **Circle of Eight** summoned by Mordenkainen at endgame. All nine die crossing the rift.
- **Lady of Pain** puts players in mini-Maze to rest and level to 20.
- **Jergal** is the final cliffhanger. Lord of the End of Everything.
- **Players can join Vecna.** This is a valid path. Account for it.
